So what?

  1. It is not a pirate bay. Rows link to HIBP and to reporting. If the ticker dies, the catalog is still the useful part. We do not mirror dumps or GTA clips.
  2. Not every leak is a hack. CyberLeek’s token traded on Aug 15 and the footage landed on Aug 18 with QR codes for the coin. The leak was the advertisement. That is on this tape as news, not as files.
  3. HIBP is the honest number. A Google search for “leak” is junk. The catalog is verified public breaches. PwnCount can overlap people across dumps.
  4. The useful signal is velocity. A quiet week of new catalog rows is information. A week of new “leak” headlines — including a $15M GTA token — is information.

How to read a leak

Three things, or it is gossip: a named source, a date, a public write-up. Credential listings on a forum are alleged until a company or a catalog like HIBP says so. Do not download “the build.”
